# Experience Documentation
There are a few changes in how the experience points are gained in combat. The idea is to make the leveling experience (pun intended) less frustrating, by increasing the amount of experience points provided by the game.   
  

## Changes
- A fainted pokemon will give 30% more experience than the original game  
- Capturing a pokemon will give half of the experience points you would get by defeating it

## Balancing
A way to balance the increased experience gained throughout the game is necessary to prevent it from becoming too easy.

To achieve this, the obedience mechanic has been extended to all Pokémon instead of applying only to traded Pokémon.

A Pokémon will begin to disobey if its level is at least two levels higher than the highest-level Pokémon of the next Gym Leader (or the Champion). In-game, however, the displayed level cap is one level lower than the actual limit, effectively giving the player one additional level of grace before Pokémon start disobeying.

The table below shows the chance of a Pokémon disobeying based on how far it exceeds the current level cap.
| Overlevel | Disobey chances |
| :---: | :---: |
| Leader Max Level | 0% |
| +1 | 0% |
| +2 | 20% |
| +3 | 40% |
| +4 | 60% |
| +5 | 80% |
| >+5 | 100% |
